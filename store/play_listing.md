## App name (max 30)
`Trapster: Chess Opening Traps`  ← 29 chars

The earlier name, `Trapster: Traps & Gambits`, carried no occurrence of
"chess" at all. Play weights the title above every other indexed field, so the
app was unreachable on the query that matters most and surfaced only on the
brand term. `Trapster: Chess Traps & Gambits` is 31 chars — one over the limit —
which is why the wording lands on "Opening" instead of "& Gambits".

## Full description (max 4000)

Trapster is the fastest way to learn the chess opening traps and gambits that win games. Study a huge library of **real, hand-picked traps** — from the Fried Liver and Légal's Mate to the Danish Gambit and Blackburne Shilling — then drill them until they're second nature.

Whether you're a beginner who keeps falling for the same tricks or a club player hunting for a sharper opening repertoire, Trapster turns opening theory into muscle memory.

▶ WHY PLAYERS LOVE TRAPSTER
• A huge library of hand-picked chess traps, gambits and opening tricks
• Learn the idea behind every move — not just memorization
• Practice Mode: play the trap yourself, move by move
• Avoid-the-Trap Mode: train to escape when the trap is set for YOU
• Built-in engine analysis with an evaluation bar and best-move hints
• Play vs a tunable Stockfish opponent, or 1v1 pass-and-play with a friend
• Search traps by name or by the moves on the board
• Track your progress: wins, streaks and traps learned
• 100% offline — study anywhere, no account, no sign-up
• Available in English, French, Spanish and Arabic

▶ LEARN THE OPENINGS THAT MATTER
Italian Game, Ruy López, Sicilian Defense, Scandinavian, Queen's Gambit, King's Gambit, Danish Gambit, Englund Gambit and dozens more — each with the classic traps every player should know, on both sides of the board.

▶ TRAIN LIKE A TACTICIAN
Chess is won and lost in the first ten moves more often than you think. Trapster helps you:
• Spring winning traps against unprepared opponents
• Recognize and sidestep the traps aimed at you
• Understand common checkmate patterns and tactical motifs
• Build a dangerous, practical opening repertoire

Download Trapster and start winning more games from move one.
